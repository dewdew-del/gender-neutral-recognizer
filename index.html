How it works

      
    
        
  1. Read. The file is turned into plain text: .txt directly, .docx with mammoth.js, .pdf with pdf.js.
    2. 
        
  2. Scan. A finite automaton walks through the text one letter at a time. The 24 terms are paths through a tree of 125 states, so every word is checked in a single pass. It ignores upper and lower case and only counts whole words, so “he” is not found inside “the”.
    3. 
        
  3. Report. Each hit is counted, highlighted in your text, and paired with a neutral suggestion. Nothing is rewritten.
    4. 
      

      
Built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript (no framework). Matching uses a hand-built DFA, not regular expressions.

Why it matters

      
Words like “chairman” or “fireman” quietly assume who does a job. Inclusive wording makes policies, job posts and reports welcome to everyone, and it is easy to miss in your own writing. This tool points out the terms so a person can decide what to change.
